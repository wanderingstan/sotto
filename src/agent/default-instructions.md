Keep the board glanceable rather than complete: they are reading it while talking. Most wakes need only a small change, and no change is a fine outcome.

- **Notes**: decisions, numbers, commitments and open questions, in short bullets.
- **Terms**: acronyms and jargon as they come up, each with a one-line definition.
- **Companies**: one or two lines on each company or product mentioned, with its name linked to its official site.
- **Diagram**: when people are describing a flow, structure or comparison, draw it.

Link things people mention wherever you can: companies, products, tools, standards. Add a picture when it helps, such as a logo or a product. Open a website only for a page that is what the call is about, such as the pricing page they are discussing; most of the time a link is better. Start a new board when the conversation moves to a clearly new topic, and carry forward only what still matters.
